Who designs a DAW like this? We did. Tape machine + console, modeled

No undo. No edit window.
Rewind to the top.

Every pass is destructive. Every take is for keeps. Punch in on the fly, then rewind and go again. That’s how the hits were made. We modeled both the 24-track tape machine and the 32-channel inline console, circuit by circuit, from the original manuals and schematics, then added a rack of classic outboard. Built to be played, not clicked.

01 · TAPE

The machine, modeled

Record, repro and bias electronics on every channel, heads and tape, plus real reel physics. 7.5, 15 and 30 IPS.

02 · CONSOLE

The console, modeled

32 inline strips, 24 buses and six sends, with EQ and dynamics modeled from the original schematics.

03 · AUTOMATION

Faders that ride with the tape

Moving faders locked to tape position. Off, Read, Write, Touch, Latch and Trim, with eight VCA groups.

04 · OUTBOARD

A rack worth patching

Vintage reverb, delay and compressor units, VST3 and AU slots, and a full patchbay you cable by hand.

Native on Apple silicon Made for 60-inch 4K screens TASCAM Studio Bridge: 24 in, 24 out, transport control
