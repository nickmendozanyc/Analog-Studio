01 · TAPE

Reels that move like reels

Every reel turns at tape speed over its own pack radius, so a full supply crawls while an empty take-up spins. A smooth servo transport eases into winds and stops without a jolt.

  • 2500 ft on 10.5-inch reels, packed to the flange
  • 7.5, 15 and 30 IPS, with vari-speed of ±20 %
  • Lifters, pinch roller and capstan that behave like the real thing
  • Autolocator with locate points and auto punch
02 · CONSOLE

An inline desk, channel for channel

Every strip carries what the original did, laid out where your hands expect it.

  • 24 independent bus-assign keys with odd / even pan and a BUS LVL rotary
  • Sweepable three-band EQ modelled from the original schematic
  • Six sends: two mono, a stereo effect send and a stereo cue
  • Full monitor section with cue mixes, talkback and solo in place
03 · AUTOMATION

Faders that ride with the tape

The mix is locked to tape position, not a timeline. Rewind, locate or punch, and the faders follow on their own.

  • Off, Read, Write, Touch, Latch and Trim on every fader
  • Eight VCA groups with mute and solo
  • Undo for every pass
04 · OUTBOARD AND PATCHBAY

Patch it like you mean it

Open the patchbay full screen and drag cables from any output to any input. Normals pass signal until you break them.

  • Vintage reverb, delay, multi-effects and compressor units with working front panels
  • VST3 and AU plugin slots
  • Click any unit to open it full screen
